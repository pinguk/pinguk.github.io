Hi, I'm Ingu. I build products at the intersection of AI, finance, and everyday habits.

A few things I believe in.

Empathy is part of the product. If people feel understood, they give you their time. That matters more than adding another feature.

I like to move fast. I’d rather put something in people’s hands in a few weeks and learn from what they actually do than spend a quarter debating what they might do.

And I’ve learned to be comfortable with constraints. I’ve worked in a warzone where basic market data barely existed, and I’ve bootstrapped to 10K users. You learn to be resourceful when there’s no other option. I think that’s usually when the best work starts.
